EveryVoyage feature FAQs

Questions aboutwhat the app can do?

The essentials about tracking, journalling, places, privacy and future route planning.

What can I do with EveryVoyage?
Track road trips with GPS, add photos and Moments, group driving days into a Voyage, record expenses, discover useful places and share journeys with the audience you choose.
Does EveryVoyage work without a mobile signal?
Yes. EveryVoyage can continue recording your route without signal and upload the Trip when a connection is available again.
Can I add a road trip after it has happened?
Yes. Add a past Trip, add Moments and bring separate driving days together inside a complete Voyage.
Can I keep my exact location private?
Yes. Audience controls, location privacy and delayed sharing let you decide who sees a journey and when it appears.
Does EveryVoyage include route planning?
Not yet. Full route planning and itinerary building are planned for later. Tracking, journalling, place discovery, the feed and privacy controls are available now.
Where can I learn more about each feature?
See the dedicated road trip tracker, road trip journal, road trip inspiration and stops and places pages.
