Road trip tracker FAQ

Questions before you start driving?

Everything you need to know about recording a road trip with EveryVoyage.

How does the EveryVoyage road trip tracker work?
Start a Trip in the EveryVoyage app and tap record. The app uses GPS to follow your route in the background while you drive. When you finish, the route is saved with your photos, Moments and Trip details.
Does road trip tracking work without mobile signal?
Yes. EveryVoyage can continue recording your route when you have no signal and upload the Trip when your connection returns.
Can I add a road trip after I have completed it?
Yes. You can create a past Trip manually, choose the route and date, and EveryVoyage helps rebuild a likely route between those places.
Can I keep my road trip private?
Yes. You control who can see your journey, and you can hide exact start and end locations when you share a Trip.
Can I add photos and notes to my tracked route?
Yes. Add photos, notes and Moments during or after the journey so each memory stays connected to where it happened.
