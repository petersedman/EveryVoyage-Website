Motorhome stop FAQs

Questions beforeExplore launches?

The current plan for stops, filters and future route planning.

What types of motorhome stops will EveryVoyage show?
The upcoming Explore map is being built around overnight stops, parking, practical services and service points. That includes campsites, aires, motorhome parking, drinking water, grey-water disposal, toilet disposal, LPG, laundry and other useful places.
Can I filter places by facilities?
Yes. Filter by amenities such as toilets, showers, drinking water, disposal, electricity, laundry, pets, swimming pools and large-vehicle access, plus nearby activities including swimming, hiking, cycling, kayaking and sightseeing.
Are stops available in EveryVoyage now?
Yes. Stops, parking, services, service points, amenity filters and nearby activity filters are available in EveryVoyage.
How will EveryVoyage keep place information useful?
The long-term advantage is the connection to real road trips. When place reviews launch, a genuine visit can lead naturally to a recent photo, rating or practical update, helping the next person make a better decision.
Will EveryVoyage include route planning?
Stop discovery is the first planning layer. Later work can connect saved places into days and routes shaped around useful stops, highlights and the kind of journey you want.
How does this work with the road trip tracker?
The road trip tracker already records where you are travelling. That creates the foundation for suggesting useful places around the current position, direction and journey rather than treating stop discovery as a completely separate task.
