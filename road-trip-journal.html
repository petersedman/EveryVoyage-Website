Road trip journal FAQs

Questions beforeyou start?

The essentials about recording, writing and bringing a journey together.

What is a road trip journal app?
A road trip journal app keeps the route, photos, notes and memories from a journey together. EveryVoyage uses Trips for the driving route, Moments for photos and notes, and a Voyage for the complete multi-day road trip.
Is EveryVoyage also a travel journal app?
Yes. Road trips are the current specialism, but the result is a digital travel journal or travel diary built around the places, days and memories from the journey.
Can I create the journal after the trip?
Yes. Add a past Trip, create Moments from photos you already have, then set the Voyage dates so the relevant entries are collected together.
What is the difference between a Trip and a Voyage?
A Trip is one driving day or leg. A Voyage is the full adventure and can contain several Trips and Moments across multiple days.
Can I keep my road trip journal private?
Yes. You choose whether a journey remains private, is shared with friends or is made visible more widely.
How does the journal relate to the road trip tracker?
The road trip tracker records the route. The journal adds the photos, notes and day-by-day story around that route.
