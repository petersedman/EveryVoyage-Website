Road trip inspiration FAQs

Questions aboutdiscovering road trips?

How the feed, real routes, place discovery, privacy and future planning fit together.

What is a road trip inspiration app?
A road trip inspiration app helps you discover where to go through real journeys, routes, places and travel stories. EveryVoyage keeps the route, stops and Moments together so you can understand how a trip actually unfolded.
Can I follow other travellers in EveryVoyage?
Yes. Follow people, see the journeys they choose to share and keep up with new Trips and Moments in the EveryVoyage feed.
Can I discover motorhome and campervan road trip routes?
Yes. Public Voyages show how separate Trips, stops and Moments fit together across a complete road trip, giving you ideas for routes and places to explore.
Does EveryVoyage plan the route for me?
Not yet. Full routing and itinerary planning are not available today. POI discovery, stop filters, the feed, tracking and journalling are available now, while deeper route planning comes later.
How does EveryVoyage help keep place information useful?
Travellers can add ratings, photos and practical updates after real visits. Connecting place information to genuine road trips helps the next traveller make a more informed choice.
Can I share a road trip without revealing my exact location?
Yes. EveryVoyage includes privacy controls that let you hide exact locations, keep journeys private or share updates later.
How does this connect to the road trip tracker and journal?
Discovery helps you decide where to go. The road trip tracker records the real route, and the road trip journal keeps the photos, Moments and driving days together afterwards.
